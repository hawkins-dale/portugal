beach

Take the 502 line from Porto center straight down Avenida de Boavista to the Castelo Do Queijo stop, which is right on the beach.  **13 minutes**.  Note that Google maps are wildly wrong.  Use https://stcp.pt/en/plan-a-trip

Or stay on one stop to the Anemone statue at Matosinhos beach

In theory there are great simple seafood restaurants on the beaches
