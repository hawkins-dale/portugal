Casa Balthazar in Lisbon, Portugal, is an exceptional, highly rated historic boutique bed-and-breakfast with an overall rating of around ==**4.8 out of 5 stars**== across major review platforms like [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d2578274-Reviews-Casa_Balthazar-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html) and [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/pt/casa-balthazar.html).

Location & Atmosphere

- **Address:** Rua do Duque 26, Lisboa, Portugal

- **Neighborhood:** Downtown Chiado/Santa Maria Maior, tucked away on a quiet, narrow side street near Rossio Square and the Carmo Convent.

- **Vibe:** A tranquil, home-like family property owned by the descendants of the founder of Lisbon's oldest pastry shop, [Confeitaria Nacional](https://www.confeitarianacional.com/). Guests frequently praise it as a peaceful oasis amid the bustling city. [1](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d2578274-Reviews-Casa_Balthazar-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html), [2](https://www.kimkim.com/h/casa-balthazar-lisbon-portugal), [3](https://www.booking.com/hotel/pt/casa-balthazar.html), [4](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/destinations/europe/portugal/lisbon/hotels/casa-balthazar-hotel/), [5](https://www.tripadvisor.ie/ShowUserReviews-g189158-d2578274-r1057380134-Casa_Balthazar-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html)]

Key Guest Praises (Pros)

- **Outstanding Breakfast & Treats:** Daily breakfast features fresh warm pastries, breads, and world-class _pastéis de nata_ supplied directly from Confeitaria Nacional. Fresh cakes, coffee, and an honesty bar are available in common areas. [1](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d2578274-Reviews-or10-Casa_Balthazar-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html), [2](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/destinations/europe/portugal/lisbon/hotels/casa-balthazar-hotel/)]

- **Service:** Staff members (such as Xavier and Ruth) receive rave reviews for being exceptionally friendly, welcoming, and accommodating. [1](https://m.yelp.com/biz/casa-balthazar-lisboa), [2](https://www.kimkim.com/h/casa-balthazar-lisbon-portugal)]

- **Amenities:** Features a small year-round heated outdoor pool, garden, terrace, and a spa area complete with a hot tub and sauna. [1](https://www.expedia.com/Lisbon-District-Hotels-Casa-Balthazar.h49172970.Hotel-Information), [2](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/destinations/europe/portugal/lisbon/hotels/casa-balthazar-hotel/)]

- **Rooms:** Spacious, uniquely styled rooms equipped with antiques, comfortable beds, Nespresso machines, and in some cases, private terraces with outdoor hot tubs and striking castle/city views. [1](https://www.tripadvisor.ca/Hotel_Feature-g189158-d2578274-zft1-Casa_Balthazar.html), [2](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/destinations/europe/portugal/lisbon/hotels/casa-balthazar-hotel/), [3](https://m.yelp.com/biz/casa-balthazar-lisboa)]

Common Criticisms (Cons)

- **Noise & Thin Walls:** Because it is a historic converted house, some guests note that walls can be thin and upper-floor movement or hallway sounds might disturb light sleepers (top-floor rooms are recommended). [1](https://m.yelp.com/biz/casa-balthazar-lisboa)]

- **Access & Stairs:** Tucked up a hill on a steep pedestrian street, getting to the property or nearby metro stops involves navigating stairs and steep inclines, which can be difficult with heavy luggage. [1](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d2578274-Reviews-or10-Casa_Balthazar-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html)]

- **Off-Season Inconsistencies:** A few isolated off-season reviews mentioned minor maintenance snags, such as chilly bathrooms in winter or less attentive upkeep compared to the peak summer season. [1](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Feature-g189158-d2578274-zft1-Casa_Balthazar.html), [2](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d2578274-Reviews-or10-Casa_Balthazar-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html)]
