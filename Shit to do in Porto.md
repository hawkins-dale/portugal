**[[Miradouro da Serra do Pilar]]**
Walk across the bridge and watch the sunset

Also the [[Parque das Virtudes]] for the view in the afternoon.  

**Explore the historic old city and riverside of Vila Nova de Gaia.**

**Avenida dos Aliados** 
Porto's top urban street, where the city goes to work in elaborate buildings

**Igreja do Carmo** spectacular tile facade depicting the founding of the Carmelite Order

**Stroll the Cais da Ribeira** 
Porto's picturesque riverfront, with arcades and colorful traditional homes.

 **São Francisco Church** Gothic church dripping with Baroque gold.

**São Bento Train Station** Entry hall decorated with impressive _azulejo_ (tile) murals.

**Porto Cathedral** Huge church overlooking the town, with fine _azulejo_-decorated cloister and otherwise dull interior.

**Rua de Santa Catarina** Main shopping drag, with Art Nouveau and Art Deco landmarks.

**Ribeira District** (UNESCO works heritage site) , includes Dom Luis I bridge

**Misericórdia Church and Museum** Church connected to a former hospital, now home to a fine exhibit showing the good works and treasures of Porto's all-powerful charity.

**Bonfim**. Neighborhood east of downtown.  Non-touristy.  Working.  Great market.  20-minute walk from the river

 **Bolhão Market** Lively produce and meat market.
 
**Foz do Douro** : the beach!  17th-century fortress /. Sunsets.

**[[Serralves]] Foundation Contemporary Art Museum and Park** Sprawling park with impressive museum, Art Deco mansion, and relaxing grounds.
