#### **Alfa Pendular** is the high-speed train from Lisbon to Porto: 
maybe book in advance
 Only book through the **official site**: [cp.pt](https://www.cp.pt/)

There are two stations to leave Lisboa from
The train in the direction of Braga

Lisbon trains depart from the Santa Apolônia station , On the Blue line and the end. 

**get a seat on the left side going to Porto** to see the cool bridges and shit coming in to Porto

Porto stations:  first is Porto Campanhǎ.  You can get off here and transfer (switch platforms) to a local train to Sǎo Bento station, which is in the center.

To get a Lisbon-to-Porto ticket, register on the *Comboios de Portugal* web page IN ADVANCE. 

Then buy a ticket to Porto - Sǎo Bento:

Choose an IC (inter city) or better AP (Alfa Pendular)

Choose "Promo ticket". 

Ignore "details for tax purposes"
