### Food

People start eating dinner at 7:30, and don't really start until 8:00 or 9:00.
**Make a res** if there's a particular place.   
But if you show up at 7:00, or even 7:30, when the locals may not show up until 9:00, you may have a chance.

*Couvert*  is a few Euros.  You can say no.  Each restaurant is dfifferent. 

The food is fresh, simple, and not overloaded with sauces. 

**DRINK ALL THE LOCAL WINE**:  the 5€ bottles are great

#### Weather

**It** **will rain**.  Umbrellas are available from street vendors.  If you have an umbrella, it's fun to walk around.

#### Transport
- Porto is **dense**.  Walk everywhere.
- [[Buses in Porto]] are excellent.  Use https://stcp.pt/en/plan-a-trip
- The [[Metro in Porto]] is cool: most lines are mostly above ground
- Or ride bikes!  Lots of bike lanes. Or an [e-bike tour](https://www.bajabikes.eu/en/porto-highlights-tour/)!
- Bolt is better than Uber.  It's the local (cheaper) version.

Car rental isn't bad!
