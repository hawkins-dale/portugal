45 minutes on the train from [[Lisbon]].  2 €.

Sintra is a magical resort town in the foothills of the mountains of Portugal, located about 15 miles (24 km) northwest of Lisbon. Famous for its cool microclimate, romantic 19th-century architecture, and forested hills, it features fairy-tale landmarks like the brightly colored **Pena National Palace**, the mystical **Quinta da Regaleira**, and the ancient **Moorish Castle**.

The whole town is a UNESCO world Heritage site. 

Top things to see:

- **Pena Palace:** A whimsical, brightly colored hilltop castle blending Gothic, Renaissance, and Moorish styles.  
> [!warning] buy tickets online in advance

- **Quinta da Regaleira:** A mysterious estate featuring hidden tunnels, grottoes, and the famous Initiation Well.  Ten minute walk from the center.

- **Moorish Castle:** Dramatic medieval stone battlements snaking along the high mountain ridges.

- **Sintra National Palace:** Located in the historic town center, known for its iconic twin chimneys and exquisite tilework.

- **Monserrate Palace:** An exotic villa surrounded by lush botanical gardens.
