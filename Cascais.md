Cascais is ==a vibrant coastal resort town in Portugal==, located about 20 miles west of Lisbon along the Estoril Coast. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cascais), [2](https://www.expedia.com/Cascais.dx4896), [3](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Tourism-g189154-Cascais_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal-Vacations.html)]

Top Things to Do

- **Beaches:** Relax on golden sand spots like Praia da Rainha right in the town center, or head to the wilder Praia do Guincho for surfing and high waves. [1](https://www.mapleleopard.com/2025/06/3-days-cascais-portugal-guide.html), [2](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Cascais)]

- **Boca do Inferno:** Walk or bike along the coast to see this dramatic cliff chasm where Atlantic waves crash into rock arches. [1](https://www.expedia.com/Things-To-Do-In-Cascais.d4896.Travel-Guide-Activities), [2](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Cascais), [3](https://www.mapleleopard.com/2025/06/3-days-cascais-portugal-guide.html)]

- **Historic Sites:** Visit the 15th-century Cidadela de Cascais (Cascais Fortress) and the opulent Condes de Castro Guimarães Museum. [1](https://www.expedia.com/Things-To-Do-In-Cascais.d4896.Travel-Guide-Activities), [2](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Cascais), [3](https://www.expedia.com/Cascais.dx4896)]

- **Coastal Promenade:** Stroll the scenic _Paredão_ walkway that connects Cascais to neighboring Estoril. [1](https://www.cascais-portugal.com/Guides/cascais-things-to-see-sightseeing.html)]

Travel Tips

- **Getting There:** Take a budget-friendly 30 to 40-minute commuter train from Lisbon's Cais do Sodré station. [1](https://blog.cattiecoylephotography.com/cascais-portugal-favorite-spots-europe/), [2](https://youshouldgohere.com/2020/02/perfect-long-weekend-in-cascais/), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VtnomLBqUUs&t=25)]

- **Best Time to Visit:** Go during the shoulder seasons of late spring (May to June) or early autumn (September) for great weather and fewer crowds. [1](https://www.cascais-portugal.com/index.html)]
