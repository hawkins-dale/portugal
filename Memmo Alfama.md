### Blather From The Hotel

Tucked away within Lisbon’s enchanting Alfama district, this intimate boutique hotel captures the city’s vibrant essence. Situated in the oldest part of Lisbon, Memmo Alfama resides in a charming 19th-century building offering 42 rooms. Stepping inside, you’ll find yourself in a world of textures, where thick stone walls blend with rustic blankets and wooden bed frames, creating a warm and tactile experience. However, the crowning jewel of this hotel is undoubtedly its rooftop terrace, which features an inviting infinity pool and a stylish lounge area. From this elevated vantage point, guests can savor sweeping views of the Tagus River, São Jorge Castle, and the iconic terracotta rooftops—an ideal spot to enjoy a glass of Vinho Verde while basking in Lisbon’s sunset.
