### Lisbon airport to city center
Easy! There are several options:
We will arrive in Terminal 1.  

**Metro**: If your hotel is close to a Metro, this is the choice. The red line connects to the other lines.  Big RED entrance. Note the artwork. 
Tickets: same ticket as local. You get a card: Viva Viagem.  The cart costs half a Euro. 
* Single ticket.  
* 24-hour ticket (included bus)
* Zapping!  can save a little money  
Tap on "without reloadable card.  Choose TWO. Keep the receipt.
Badge in AND OUT.  20-30 minutes to central

**Autocarros**: buses. NO!  Aerobus is still not working.  So NO TO BUS.

**Taxis**. The stand is well-identified.  Ensure that the driver uses the meter. Most are honest. 

**TVDE**: Uber and Bolt.  Walk to the "Kiss & Fly".
