Porto is generally very safe, but certain peripheral neighborhoods are best avoided due to drug activity.
- **Bairro do Aleixo:** Located in the Lordelo do Ouro area, it is historically one of the most notorious spots for drug activity.
- **Cerco do Porto:** Situated in the eastern Campanhã zone, it can be unsafe for wandering tourists
- **Pasteleira Nova & Lagarteiro:** Located on the western and far eastern fringes of the city, respectively, these areas frequently appear on locals' warning lists.
