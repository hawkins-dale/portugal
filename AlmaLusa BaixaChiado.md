### Overview & Ratings

- **Overall Rating**: 4.7 / 5 stars across major platforms like [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d9596331-Reviews-AlmaLusa_Baixa_Chiado-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html) and Tablet Hotels.

### Common Complaints (Cons)

- **Water & Space**: A few guests mention that shower designs can splash water onto the bathroom floor, and standard rooms are compact due to the constraints of the historic landmark structure. [[1](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotel_Review-g189158-d9596331-Reviews-AlmaLusa_Baixa_Chiado-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html), [2](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g189158-d9596331-Reviews-AlmaLusa_Baixa_Chiado-Lisbon_Lisbon_District_Central_Portugal.html)]

- **In-Room Tech**: Some visitors find the automated in-room smartphone/technology setup slightly unintuitive. [[1](https://www.cntraveler.com/hotels/lisbon/almalusa-baixa-chiado)]

- **Minor Service Inconsistencies**: A small handful of past reviews noted occasional communication or service gaps during peak check-in times. [1](https://travel.usnews.com/hotels/review-almalusa_baixachiado-lisbon-portugal-154845/)]
