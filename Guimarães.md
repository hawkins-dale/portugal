The **[Palace of the Dukes of Braganza](https://emrld.cc/re?campaign_id=108&marker=132667&p=3965&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.getyourguide.com%2Fguimaraes-l4960%2Fguimaraes-palace-of-the-dukes-of-braganca-entry-ticket-t917386%2F%3Fcurrency%3DUSD%26psrc%3Dpartner_api&trs=163609&trace_id=Zz1c792a05f40a4f41b598ad1-132667&install_type=am_partner_content&product_type=emerald&journey_id=-jX7x7l6xrTBqZ2Xh-xZI&promo_kind=tp_long_with_tooltip_widget&page_url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.winalist.com%2Fblog%2Fportugal%2Fvinho-verde%2Fwhat-to-do-guimaraes-portugal%3Futm_source%3Dgoogleads%26utm_medium%3Dcpc%26utm_campaign%3Dpmax-catgc-langen-locukieuscaau%26utm_content%3D%26utm_term%3D%26origin%3Dgpmax%26gad_source%3D1%26gad_campaignid%3D23503790558%26gbraid%3D0AAAAADL6JsDvAuA2guvCd7WK62VRIOcHq%26gclid%3DCjwKCAjwnvTUBhBoEiwAZNDxZ8tYmUuJuAUCpHRreTYFGI74CjOxUvG-PiCx5k4ypDcbmiwu1UHNdhoC5isQAvD_BwE&is_tooltip=true&tooltip_subtype=tour&promo_subkind=tooltip_desktop_general)** is an impressive 15th-century palace reflecting medieval and Baroque architectural influences. Inside, collections of tapestries, furniture, and armaments immerse you in Portugal’s noble history. This palace is one of the most visited sites in **Guimarães** and warrants an extended stop.


The **Castle of Guimarães** is a powerful symbol of Portuguese **independence**. This 10th-century medieval fortress played a key role in the country’s history. Located on a hill, it offers breathtaking views of the city. Don’t miss the chance to enter and discover the walls and towers that once protected the future **first king of Portugal**, Dom Afonso Henriques.


**[Largo da Oliveira](https://emrld.cc/re?campaign_id=84&marker=132667&p=2076&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.booking.com%2Fsearchresults.en-gb.html%3Fdest_id%3D6796814%26dest_type%3Dhotel&trs=163609&trace_id=Zz1c792a05f40a4f41b598ad1-132667&install_type=am_partner_content&product_type=emerald&journey_id=t7fofsx0PPPvjcbnzck6q&promo_kind=tp_long_with_tooltip_widget&page_url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.winalist.com%2Fblog%2Fportugal%2Fvinho-verde%2Fwhat-to-do-guimaraes-portugal%3Futm_source%3Dgoogleads%26utm_medium%3Dcpc%26utm_campaign%3Dpmax-catgc-langen-locukieuscaau%26utm_content%3D%26utm_term%3D%26origin%3Dgpmax%26gad_source%3D1%26gad_campaignid%3D23503790558%26gbraid%3D0AAAAADL6JsDvAuA2guvCd7WK62VRIOcHq%26gclid%3DCjwKCAjwnvTUBhBoEiwAZNDxZ8tYmUuJuAUCpHRreTYFGI74CjOxUvG-PiCx5k4ypDcbmiwu1UHNdhoC5isQAvD_BwE&is_tooltip=true&tooltip_subtype=search&promo_subkind=tooltip_desktop_general)** is one of the most beautiful and iconic squares in the historic center of **Guimarães**, surrounded by remarkably well-preserved **medieval buildings** that transport visitors back in time. Named after the ancient olive tree that once stood in its center, the square is home to the stunning **Church of Our Lady of the Olive Tree (Igreja de Nossa Senhora da Oliveira)**, a key religious and architectural landmark. The adjacent **Padrão do Salado**, a Gothic shrine commemorating a 14th-century battle, adds to the square’s historical significance.

Today, Largo da Oliveira is a vibrant gathering place where locals and visitors alike enjoy coffee or wine on sunny terraces, soaking up the ambiance of one of Portugal’s most charming plazas. Whether you’re people-watching, admiring the architecture, or simply pausing to relax, it’s a must-see when exploring Guimarães.
